# MindDock

**Your knowledge. Your space. Your rules.**

MindDock is a self-hosted personal knowledge management and productivity workspace inspired by Notion and Obsidian.

The goal is simple: create a powerful, flexible, and distraction-free environment where you can organize notes, connect ideas, manage projects, and build your own knowledge base — while keeping complete control over your data.

## Vision

A personal workspace that combines the flexibility of Notion with the knowledge-linking capabilities of Obsidian, without relying on third-party cloud services.

**Self-hosted by design. Built for ownership.**
